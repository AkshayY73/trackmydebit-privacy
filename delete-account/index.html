Delete your Track My Debit account

Track My Debit · Android and iOS app

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to grievance@trackmydebit.com from the email address you use to sign in to Track My Debit.
  2. Use the subject Delete my account. You don't need to give a reason.
  3. We delete your account and the data listed below within 7 business days, and reply by email to confirm.
Email a deletion request

If you no longer have access to that email address, write from another address and tell us the email you signed up with. We may ask you to confirm it is your account before deleting it.

What is deleted

DataWhere it is stored
Your sign-in account (email, sign-in method, password if you used email)Google Firebase Authentication
Your profile: name, email, age, sign-up time, trial statusGoogle Cloud Firestore and our sign-in server
Your session record: session ID, sign-in time and device descriptionGoogle Cloud Firestore

Deleting the account also signs you out on every device.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Server logs: our sign-in server writes a log line when an account is created or deleted. It contains only your account's random ID, not your name or email, and is removed automatically when the hosting provider rotates its logs, within 30 days.
  • Your request email: kept only until we have confirmed the deletion to you, then deleted.
  • Nothing else from your account is kept.

Data on your phone

Your SMS messages, transactions, SMS formats and categories are never uploaded, so we have no copy to delete. To remove them from your phone, open the app and go to Settings → Privacy & consent → erase all data, or uninstall the app.

Using the app without an account

If you never created an account ("Use without an account"), nothing is stored online. Erasing data in the app or uninstalling it removes everything.
