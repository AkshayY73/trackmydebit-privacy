Track My Debit Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026 · App package: com.budgetapp.smstrack

In short: Track My Debit reads your bank SMS alerts to record your spending, and does all of that on your phone. Your messages and transactions are never uploaded. If you create an account, we store only what is needed to sign you in: your name, email, age and sign-in details.

Who we are

Track My Debit ("the app", "we") is an expense tracker for Android and iOS. For any privacy question or request, write to grievance@trackmydebit.com. We reply within 7 business days.

Data that stays on your phone

The following is processed and stored only on your device, in the app's local database. It is not sent to us or to anyone else:

  • SMS messages the app reads to find bank alerts
  • Transactions (amount, date, account, category, notes)
  • The SMS formats you teach the app, your categories and your settings

How the app uses SMS

  • Android: with your permission (READ_SMS and RECEIVE_SMS), the app reads your inbox and new incoming messages to detect bank debit and credit alerts and turn them into transactions. Messages are read on the device only.
  • iPhone: iOS does not let apps read SMS. If you set up an Apple Shortcuts automation, it passes the text of a bank alert to the app, which processes it on the device.
  • SMS reading is optional. You can decline or turn it off at any time in the app (Settings → Privacy & consent) or in your phone's settings, and keep adding transactions by hand.

Data we collect if you create an account

An account is optional; you can use the app without one, in which case nothing is stored online. If you sign in with Google, Apple or email, we store:

DataWhy
Name and email addressTo identify your account and let you sign in
Sign-in method (Google, Apple or email)To sign you in the same way next time
AgeTo confirm you are 15 or older
Account creation time and trial statusTo show your free-trial period
A random session ID, sign-in time and a short device description (for example "android · Linux; Android 14")To keep your account signed in on one device at a time and sign out older devices

Passwords for email accounts are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store them.

Where account data is stored

Account data is stored with service providers that process it only on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore)
  • Render, which hosts the app's sign-in server

Their servers may be located outside your country. All data sent between the app and these services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

What we do not do

  • We do not upload your SMS messages or transactions.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties, other than the service providers above.
  • We do not link to your bank account.

How long we keep data

Account data is kept while your account exists and deleted when you ask us to delete the account. Data on your phone stays until you erase it in the app or uninstall the app.

Your choices and rights

  • Erase data on your phone: Settings → Privacy & consent → erase all data. Uninstalling the app also removes it.
  • Stop SMS reading: turn it off in Settings → Privacy & consent, or revoke the SMS permission in your phone's settings.
  • Delete your account: email grievance@trackmydebit.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete your account and its stored data within 7 business days and confirm by email.
  • Access or correct your data: you can edit your name and email in Settings → Edit profile, or write to us for a copy of what we hold.

If you are in India, you also have the rights of a Data Principal under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, including the right to raise a grievance with us at the address above and, if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

Accounts are for people aged 15 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a child under 15 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and its effective date, and tell you in the app when the change is significant.
