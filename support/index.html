Common questions

  • Free trial: every new account gets 5 days with automatic SMS logging.
  • Subscriptions: manage, change or cancel in the App Store or Google Play account settings. Cancelling stops renewal from the next billing date.
  • Restore purchases: open Settings → Subscription in the app and choose Restore.
  • iPhone automatic logging: set up the Apple Shortcut from the app's setup screen. You can delete the Shortcut at any time.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Edit Account Profile → Delete account, or see the account deletion page.
